# App Attest test vectors

All attestations are genuine Apple-root-signed objects. For each, the App Attest fraud receipt has been removed and only the outer CBOR map has been re-encoded deterministically. No receipt, risk token, private key or sensor data is included. Hex strings encode raw bytes. Use the fixture's historical verify-at UTC Unix seconds, not the current time.

- `public-ios-14.4-pair.json`: [takimoto3/app-attest, testdata/ios-14.4.json at 9d7551c](https://github.com/takimoto3/app-attest/blob/9d7551c05804d3e35d5477b5aed1d82d8f8a2035/testdata/ios-14.4.json) (MIT); same iOS device sample originally published in [veehaitch/devicecheck-appattest, src/test/resources/ios-14.4.yaml at cb26211](https://github.com/veehaitch/devicecheck-appattest/blob/cb26211f63c1e2e7949deafe2efdf352daca27fa/src/test/resources/ios-14.4.yaml) (Apache-2.0). This development credential and assertion use the same key. Verify at the Unix second containing the source's attestation timestamp `2021-01-23T12:13:33.335Z`. The assertion is untouched and is expected to advance the counter to 1.
- `public-production.json`: [uebelack/node-app-attest, test/fixtures/attestation-production.json at 493d029](https://github.com/uebelack/node-app-attest/blob/493d029e2d6a7e6bc8b4e4d032b01fb204b85c39/test/fixtures/attestation-production.json) (MIT). Verify one second after the credential certificate's notBefore (2024-02-06T21:08:56Z).
- `device-development.json`: our own test device, produced 2026-11-20 by the Galaxy iOS app's App Attest probe: iPhone 13 Pro Max (iPhone14,3), iOS 27.2.1, app ID `PPS57BF3X3.utcq.XRPL-Galaxy`, free-team Debug build without an App Attest entitlement, **development environment** (AAGUID `appattestdevelop`). The key is a development-environment key on a test device. Contents are only what a DEVICE bundle and CAPTURE memos would publish on XRPL: the receipt-stripped attestation, key ID, app ID, the §3 DEVICE-REG preimage (`registration_client_data_hex`; network 1 with placeholder ledger 1234, ledger hash `a5…a5` and account `b6…b6`, plus the app's random `reg_nonce`) and two assertions with their clientData (`"GALAXY/ASSERT/v1\0"` followed by 299 bytes of `0x00` or `0x01` as placeholder cores, so they are not decodable CAPTURE cores). Counters are 1 and 2. Verify at one second after the credential certificate's notBefore (2026-11-19T19:44:00Z). The raw objects, including Apple's receipt, stay in the gitignored `fixtures/real/appattest/`.

The two public fixtures use their upstream plaintext challenges/clientData (hashed once before being passed to the App Attest framework), not Galaxy §3 messages. They test the shared Apple verification primitives and do not claim a Galaxy DEVICE registration or XRPL CAPTURE; the device fixture's attestation is a Galaxy §3 registration and is verified through `verify_registration`. Negative tests derive from these real bytes by removing required fields, tampering with signed data or changing verification inputs; no fake Apple success path exists. The receipt-strip test inserts an opaque non-device field to check that stripping preserves all authenticated bytes.

`cases.json` holds 60 golden cases (`check` = `attestation`, `assertion`, `strip_receipt`, `counter`, `registration` or `registration_and_capture`; `expected` = `ok` or an `AttestError::code()`; `result` for successful cases). Apple objects come from the three files above; mutations cover a chain not signed by the pinned root, swapped chain order, verification before notBefore and after notAfter, other challenge, altered authData, wrong format, app ID and environment, wrong key ID, receipt present vs stripped, assertions over other clientData, changed or zero counters, raw (non-DER) signatures, trailing bytes, counter replay, manifest/bundle mismatch, account and registration-ledger binding. The `dev_unattested` cases use a labelled test-only Ed25519 key derived from a fixed seed in `crates/galaxy-attestation/src/tests/vectors.rs`; only its public key and signatures appear. The crate tests check that the file equals the generated cases and that every case yields its expected outcome; regenerate with `GALAXY_WRITE_VECTORS=1 cargo test -p galaxy-attestation vectors`.
