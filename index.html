What the verdict means.

VERIFIED means physical consistency under the named policy. It does not establish that the scene is true. Missing evidence does not mean the photo was manipulated.

Current capture profile: iPhone with LiDAR (galaxy-pc-v1m). Profiles for phones without LiDAR are on the roadmap.
