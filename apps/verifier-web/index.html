Check whether a JPG or PNG is bound to a proof under an accepted Galaxy policy.

Lookup method
64 hexadecimal characters. The verifier searches a bounded range of ledger transactions for the proof.

    Network and privacy settings
    HTTPS RPC requires CORS; WebSocket (WSS) is preferred. Plain HTTP/WS is allowed only on localhost or 127.0.0.1. Off by default. Even when enabled, the verifier never contacts a node without your consent for the selected photo. HTTPS templates must include {hash} or {index}, respectively. Leave empty to hide links.

    Galaxy checks a cryptographic policy. It does not establish that the depicted scene is true.
